# Transparent audit benchmarks

Assess WAN exposure, segmentation, IoT isolation, Guest isolation, management-plane security, port-forward hygiene, rule quality, VPN posture, DNS/DHCP posture, wireless security, device/update posture, and configuration hygiene.

Every finding includes severity, category, evidence, why it matters, confidence, recommendation, evidence type (`measured`, `reported`, `inferred`, `not_available`), and automation safety. Scores, when shown, are counts weighted by published severity—not opaque grades. Missing telemetry reduces confidence and never proves safety or rule inactivity.
